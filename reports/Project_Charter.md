## Background

Marketing organizations invest in multiple channels including Email, Paid Search, Social Media, Direct Traffic, and Display Advertising. Understanding which channels contribute most effectively to conversions is essential for maximizing marketing ROI and improving decision-making.

This project will analyze multi-touch attribution data and develop an interactive dashboard to evaluate channel effectiveness across the customer journey.

---

## Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to identify which marketing channels generate the greatest impact on customer conversions. Without proper attribution analysis, marketing budgets may be allocated inefficiently, reducing overall campaign effectiveness and return on investment.

---

## Project Goal

To develop an interactive analytics dashboard that measures marketing channel performance, conversion contribution, and return on investment (ROI) using multi-touch attribution data.

---

## Objectives

* Explore and understand customer journey data.
* Clean and prepare marketing attribution data.
* Analyze channel performance and conversion impact.
* Develop key marketing KPIs.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business users.
* Generate actionable recommendations for marketing optimization.
